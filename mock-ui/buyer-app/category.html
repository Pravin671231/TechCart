Card rules — FR-CAT-092

  • Up to the first six filterable pairs, in schema declaration order — admins choose which six by reordering the schema.
  • Fewer than six filterable fields renders only those it has (card 3 above).
  • A category marking none filterable renders the plain home-style card (PlainCategoryProductCard), unchanged at every breakpoint.
  • The limit of six is display-only — it never caps how many fields may be marked filterable.
  • Desktop (md+): 4 regions — image · title+specs · dedicated price section (bordered, tinted, holds the Add-to-Cart button) · an empty reserved gutter.
  • Mobile (< md): image left; the right column stacks the price section above the title; the spec list is dropped.
  • Every facet in the rail composes together, with sort, and pagination · FR-CAT-076
